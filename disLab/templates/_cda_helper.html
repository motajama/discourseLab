CDA prompts

Fairclough-oriented prompts

  • What textual choices carry evaluative meaning?
  • How is agency distributed?
  • What is foregrounded and backgrounded?
  • What is presupposed?
  • Which discourses are articulated together?
  • How does the text relate to wider social practice?

van Dijk-oriented prompts

  • Who has access to voice?
  • Who controls the definition of the situation?
  • How are "us" and "them" constructed?
  • Is there positive self-presentation?
  • Is there negative other-presentation?
  • How is dominance reproduced?

Actor/voice prompts

  • Who speaks?
  • Who is silenced?
  • Who is individualized?
  • Who is aggregated?
  • Who acts?
  • Who is acted upon?

Sources

  • Fairclough, N. (1992). Discourse and social change. Polity.
  • Fairclough, N. (1995). Critical discourse analysis: The critical study of language. Longman.
  • van Dijk, T. A. (2008). Discourse and power. Palgrave Macmillan.
  • Wodak, R., & Meyer, M. (Eds.). (2016). Methods of critical discourse studies (3rd ed.). SAGE.
  • Machin, D., & Mayr, A. (2012). How to do critical discourse analysis: A multimodal introduction. SAGE.
